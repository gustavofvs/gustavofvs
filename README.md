### A little about me

I'm a developer from Brazil. This profile is where I keep projects, experiments, and solutions to problems that bothered me enough to fix.

I enjoy simple interfaces, useful software, and understanding what is happening under the hood. I also use AI in some projects, usually to automate repetitive work or make a tool more useful.

Some projects are polished; others are just ideas I'm testing to see where they go.

### What you'll find here

* Tools I wanted to exist
* Experiments that became real projects
* Web, Android, and systems-related things
* Projects involving AI and automation
* Occasional sim racing and 3D printing side quests
